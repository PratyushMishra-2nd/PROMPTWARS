RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease ("Lease") is made on February 1, 2026, between Landlord and Tenant for the premises located at 123 Maple Street, Apt 4B.

1. TERM
The initial term is twelve (12) months commencing March 1, 2026. After the initial term, the Lease shall continue on a month-to-month basis until terminated.

2. RENT
Monthly rent is $2,400, due on the first day of each month. Rent paid after the third day of the month incurs a late fee equal to 10% of monthly rent plus $25 per day until paid. Late fees compound at 1.5% per month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $7,200 (three months' rent) upon signing. The deposit is non-refundable in the event of any breach of this Lease, regardless of actual damages. Landlord may retain the deposit in full at Landlord's sole discretion.

4. EARLY TERMINATION
If Tenant terminates this Lease before the end of the initial term, Tenant shall pay all remaining rent for the full initial term as liquidated damages, plus a $1,500 administrative fee.

5. LANDLORD ENTRY
Landlord may enter the premises at any time, for any reason, with or without notice to Tenant, including but not limited to inspections, repairs, showings to prospective tenants, and any other purpose Landlord deems appropriate.

6. RENEWAL
At the end of the initial term, rent may be increased in Landlord's sole discretion with no maximum cap. Tenant must provide 90 days' written notice of intent to vacate; failure to do so results in automatic renewal for another 12-month term at the increased rate.

7. REPAIRS
Tenant is responsible for all repairs and maintenance, including major systems (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, structural), regardless of cause. Landlord has no obligation to make any repairs.

8. SUBLETTING
Tenant may not sublet, assign, or allow any guest to stay more than 3 nights per month without Landlord's prior written consent, which may be withheld for any reason.

9. WAIVER OF RIGHTS
Tenant waives all rights to a jury trial and waives any defenses or counterclaims in any action brought by Landlord. Tenant agrees not to participate in any tenant association or organize with other tenants regarding building conditions.

10. ATTORNEY'S FEES
In any action arising out of this Lease, Tenant shall pay Landlord's reasonable attorney's fees regardless of outcome.

11. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of New York. Venue is exclusively in New York County.

12. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Lease constitutes the entire agreement. No oral statements by Landlord or Landlord's agents are binding.
